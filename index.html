SpartanInsight: Transforming Student Portal & SETE Data into Actionable Academic Insights

An integrated analytics and decision-support platform bridging enrollment, grade submissions, and Student Evaluation of Teaching Effectiveness (SETE) across CICS sections for proactive academic leadership.

Built for informed academic leadership

01 · INTEGRATION

Unified Portal + SETE Integration

Connects grade submission logs, subject passing rates, and SETE participation across all 20 CICS sections (1101–4105).

02 · MONITORING

Proactive KPI & Alert Monitoring

Flags late grade submissions (> 5%), low SETE response rates (< 80%), and underperforming sections (< 85%) before end-of-term deadlines.

03 · GOVERNANCE

Privacy-First Governance (RBAC)

Protects student anonymity with a minimum 5-response threshold and Role-Based Access Control tailored for the CICS Program Chair.
